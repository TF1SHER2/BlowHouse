The sound of Mobile Mardi Gras! Blow House Brass Band is a high-energy Second Line jazz ensemble based in Mobile, Alabama — the birthplace of America’s original Mardi Gras. Since 2007, Blow House has been igniting parades, balls, parties, and festivals for revelers of all kinds with their unmistakable sound. Hailed as “The Sound of Mardi Gras,” Blow House delivers a blend of classic Carnivale fare and down and dirty funk that captures the spirit, swagger, and Southern charm of Mobile Mardi Gras culminating in a unique and lively musical experience... just like the Azalea City we call home!
